## The short version

You're charged in full when you subscribe — there is no delayed-billing
free trial. If Meridian isn't right for you, cancel within **7 days** of
your first payment for a full refund, no questions asked. After 7 days,
you can still cancel any time — it stops future billing — but the current
billing period isn't refunded.

## How it works

1. **Subscribing charges you immediately.** Completing checkout creates an
   active subscription and charges your card (or other payment method)
   for the first billing period right away, processed by
   [Paystack](https://paystack.com), our payment processor.
2. **The 7-day window starts at that first charge**, not at any later
   date. It does not reset on renewal.
3. **Cancelling within the window** (Billing → Cancel & get a full
   refund, or [SUPPORT EMAIL]) refunds the full amount of that first
   charge back to your original payment method and ends your
   subscription immediately. Refunds are issued through Paystack and
   typically appear on your statement within [X] business days,
   depending on your bank or card issuer — we don't control that timing.
4. **Cancelling after the window** stops future billing but does not
   refund the current period; you keep access until the end of the
   period you already paid for.
5. **Renewal charges are not covered by this window.** The 7-day
   guarantee applies only to a subscription's first payment. If you'd
   like a different policy for renewals, that's a decision for
   [COMPANY NAME] to make explicitly, not something implied by this
   policy as written.

## What isn't covered

- Any usage-based or overage charges outside your base subscription fee
  (if/when Meridian offers those) — refund eligibility for those, if any,
  will be stated separately at the time they're introduced.
- Accounts terminated for violating the [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md)
  (acceptable-use violations, fraud, non-payment) are not entitled to a
  refund regardless of timing.

Admins can self-serve this from **Billing → Cancel & get a full refund**
inside the app while within the window. Outside the app, contact
